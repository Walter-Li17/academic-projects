# Reading references

Background and sources for the current study. The Grassmann-pooling paper is the next replication target, not a completed reproduction.

- **DINO original paper:** Caron et al. (2021). [Emerging Properties in Self-Supervised Vision Transformers](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/ICCV2021/html/Caron_Emerging_Properties_in_Self-Supervised_Vision_Transformers_ICCV_2021_paper.html).
  [Original code](https://github.com/facebookresearch/dino).
- **Latest advisor-assigned paper, next replication target:** Xing Wei, Yue Zhang, Yihong Gong, Jiawei Zhang, Nanning Zheng (2018). [Grassmann Pooling as Compact Homogeneous Bilinear Pooling for Fine-Grained Visual Classification](https://www.ecva.net/papers/eccv_2018/papers_ECCV/html/Xing_Wei_Grassmann_Pooling_for_ECCV_2018_paper.php).
- **Dataset and evaluation:** Radenović et al. (2018). [Revisiting Oxford and Paris: Large-Scale Image Retrieval Benchmarking](https://cmp.felk.cvut.cz/revisitop/).
- **PCA/whitening motivation:** Jégou and Chum (2012). [Negative Evidences and Co-occurrences in Image Retrieval: The Benefit of PCA and Whitening](https://cmp.felk.cvut.cz/~chum/papers/Jegou-ECCV12.pdf).
- **Subspace geometry:** Edelman, Arias and Smith (1998). [The Geometry of Algorithms with Orthogonality Constraints](https://math.mit.edu/~edelman/publications/geometry_of_algorithms.pdf).
